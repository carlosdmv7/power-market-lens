# Project review, November 2026

A review of the whole repository at the end of Phase 1, read the way a senior
data engineering interviewer would read it: what is weak, fragile or
unfinished, and what is missing for it to look professional-grade. Scope and
stack are those of `CLAUDE.md`; nothing here proposes changing them without a
decision.

Every number below was measured on 2026-11-14 against the repository at
`c616d55` and against `power_prod` (read-only).

## Verdict

The foundations are stronger than most portfolio projects: append-only raw
with a documented deduplication rule, UTC keys with one macro for local time,
DST-aware completeness tests, parsers tested on recorded responses, a single
writer to prod, 96% test coverage, and ADRs that record rejected alternatives.
An interviewer will find those quickly.

What they will also find is that the project was **built carefully and
operated loosely**. The eight-day silent outage is the clearest symptom, and
it is now closed (#44, #45). The remaining gaps are of the same family: things
the design promises that production does not yet do (Prefect observability,
dbt-expectations), growth that nobody measures (raw duplicates), and
guarantees that are conventions rather than enforced (branch protection,
contracts on the marts the app reads).

## How to read the list

- **Impact**: how much it changes what an expert concludes about the project,
  or how much risk it removes. High / Medium / Low.
- **Effort**: S (under an hour), M (an afternoon), L (more than a day).
- **Who**: `auto` = Python, CI, app or docs, implemented without a decision;
  `dbt` = touches SQL, seeds or dbt tests, so it goes in as a proposal for
  review; `decision` = needs a choice, a credential or a repository setting.

Items are ordered by impact against effort within each block.

## 2. Operations and reliability

| # | Item | Impact | Effort | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Prod runs are not recorded in Prefect Cloud.** `PREFECT_API_KEY` and `PREFECT_API_URL` are not repository secrets, so every Pipeline run logs "this run will not appear in Prefect Cloud" and uses a temporary local server. The README and ADR-002 present Prefect as the observability layer; in prod it currently adds retries and nothing else. | High | S | decision (two secrets) |
| 2.2 | **Raw grows without bound, mostly with duplicates.** Each run re-appends the trailing 7 days, and GitHub's delays mean two full runs most days. `raw.entsoe_generation_per_type` holds 17.0 M rows for 12.9 M distinct points (32% duplicates after three weeks of daily runs). A normal day appends about 210,000 generation rows for about 12,000 new points: 77 M rows a year for 4.5 M points. Staging is a view that ranks the whole table on every build, so build time grows with it. `power_prod` is 379 MiB; MotherDuck's free plan allows 10 GB. Options in section 7. | High | M | dbt + decision |
| 2.3 | **One missing source hour stops downstream marts from refreshing.** `dbt build` skips every model downstream of a failed test. Today that is harmless (the marts have no children), but after #27 the mix mart sits below `fct_generation_hourly`: a single hour the publisher has not released would freeze the mix page until a seed row is merged. On 2026-11-14 exactly this test failed (FR, 13 Nov, local hour 19, absent at ENTSO-E when re-requested). Options in section 7. | High | S | dbt |
| 2.4 | **The schedule runs five to seven hours late.** The cron asks for 12:37 UTC; in the two weeks before the outage the first run started between 17:20 and 20:14 UTC. Prices published at 12:45 CET reach the app in the evening. ADR-002 accepts "delayed by hours"; the measured delay deserves a line there, and an external trigger would fix it (section 7). | Medium | S–M | decision |
| 2.5 | **Retries multiply.** A failing request is tried 4 times in `with_backoff`, and the Prefect task wraps that in 3 attempts: up to 12 requests and about 16 minutes for one load that cannot succeed. Sixty such loads never fit the timeout, which is how any systemic failure (not only auth) becomes a timed-out run. A circuit breaker (stop after N consecutive failed loads) bounds it. | Medium | S | auto |
| 2.6 | **No runbook.** The incident issue says what happened, not what to do. A one-page `docs/runbook.md` (renew the token, re-run a backfill slice, verify and record a source gap, read the freshness check) turns the alert into an action. | Medium | S | auto |
| 2.7 | **dbt artifacts are thrown away.** `manifest.json` and `run_results.json` of each prod build die with the runner. Uploading them as workflow artifacts gives test history for post-mortems and is the prerequisite for slim CI (Phase 4). | Medium | S | auto |
| 2.8 | **The watchdog shares GitHub's scheduler with the pipeline** (ADR-007, known gap). GitHub disables schedules after 60 days without repository activity. An external dead-man's switch closes it. | Low | S | decision (new service) |

## 7. Proposals that need a decision

### 7.1 Raw growth (item 2.2)

- **(a) Append only what changed.** Before inserting, drop rows identical to
  the latest stored version of the same point. Raw stays append-only and keeps
  every revision; it stops storing re-pulled rows that did not change, which is
  most of the 94% of each day's rows that are re-pulls. Loader change plus a test; no dbt change. **Recommended.**
- **(b) Compact raw on a schedule**: delete all but the latest copy older than
  N days. Smaller raw, but it breaks "raw is never updated" in `CLAUDE.md` and
  deletes data.
- **(c) Leave raw, make staging incremental.** Keeps build time flat but not
  storage, and adds incremental logic to the layer that is simplest today.

### 7.2 Completeness test and late publication (item 2.3)

- **(a) Split the test by age.** Days inside the trailing window (the last 7)
  warn; older days fail. A publisher's late hour then never blocks downstream
  marts, and a gap that survives the window still turns the pipeline red.
  **Recommended**, with the seed kept for permanent gaps.
- **(b) Keep the test strict and add the seed row** for FR 2026-11-13 hour 19
  now. Consistent with ADR-006, but the pipeline stays red (and after #27 the
  mix mart stale) every time a publisher is a day late.
- **(c) `severity: warn` everywhere.** Rejected in ADR-006 for good reason.

### 7.3 Schedule delay (item 2.4)

- **(a) Accept and document it** in ADR-002. Zero cost, zero moving parts.
  **Recommended for now**: the app explains prices, it does not trade on them.
- **(b) Trigger `workflow_dispatch` from an external scheduler.** On time, but
  a new service and a token with `actions: write` stored outside GitHub.
- **(c) A Prefect Cloud schedule.** Needs a deployment; ADR-002 rules it out.

### 7.4 dbt-expectations (item 3.2)

- **(a) Use it for two or three tests that the built-in ones cannot express**
  (3.5). **Recommended**: the stack already promises it.
- **(b) Remove it** from `packages.yml` and the stack table.
